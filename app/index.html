YouTube links

Make tamescroll your YouTube.

Android sends every YouTube link to the YouTube app until you tell it otherwise. Two switches, then a test.

  1. 1
    Turn off YouTube's links

    On the page that opens, choose In your browser (on some phones: switch Open supported links off).

  2. 2
    Turn on tamescroll's links

    Same page, ours. Keep In the app, then Add link and select all four.

  3. 3
    Test it

    Opens a real link. If it lands here, you're done.

Optional

  1. YouTube on your home screen

    A tamescroll icon that opens YouTube, cleaned.

  2. Remove the YouTube app

    Opens its app info page. Uninstall is your call.

About

tamescroll is a calm browser: it opens any site, and tames the busy ones.

Free and open source, MPL-2.0. No account, no telemetry, no server in the middle. Image blur and keyword filters run on your device.

Filter rules

Blocking rules refresh themselves in the background — no reinstall when a site changes its layout.

Diagnostics

Performance numbers from your own device. Kept on this phone. Nothing is sent unless you share it.

Built on

Filter lists from EasyList and EasyPrivacy (CC BY-SA 3.0) and uBlock Origin's uAssets (GPLv3), read as data by Brave's adblock-rust engine (MPL-2.0). Models: BlazeFace and MoveNet (Apache-2.0), faceres via vladmandic/human (MIT). Shell: Tauri.

Full notices ship in the source repository's NOTICE file.
