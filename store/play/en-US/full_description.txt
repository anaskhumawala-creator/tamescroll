tamescroll is a calm web browser for the sites you already use. It opens them as cleaned versions of themselves, so you can watch, read and reply without being pulled into an endless feed.

WHAT IT REMOVES
• Short-video feeds, recommended feeds, trending and autoplay
• Sign-in nags and pop-ups
• Ads, if you choose: one switch per site blocks them

PROTECTS YOUR GAZE
Thumbnails, images and video previews are blurred on your phone before you see them. The video you choose to watch plays normally. Nothing you see is sent anywhere: the filtering runs entirely on your device.

YOU CHOOSE THE SITES
Type the sites you already use, or search the web from the box at the top. tamescroll never lists or suggests a site to you. Each removed part of a page has one plain switch, so you bring back only what you want, like comments or subscriptions.

SIMPLE ENOUGH FOR ANYONE
Big tiles, no accounts, no setup beyond typing a site's name. Links shared in your messaging apps can open here instead of in a feed app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no advertising ID
• Your settings stay on your phone
