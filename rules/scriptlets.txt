! YouTube ad-free stream, no watch-click stall. Sets one field on the
! outbound /player request so InnerTube grants a stream with no ad and no
! SABR backoff — the backoff our response-side strip cannot undo (the
! multi-second freeze owner reports #9/#12).
! Provenance (clean-room): public protobuf reverse-engineering of Google's
! own API (https://iter.ca/post/yt-adblock/), NOT uBO/AdGuard code.
! test: on a youtube.com /watch SPA click, the outbound
!   POST /youtubei/v1/player body carries
!   playbackContext.contentPlaybackContext.isInlinePlaybackNoAd:true and no
!   pre-roll plays; video still plays (player red line).
! [unverified] — needs owner phone; emulator is never served real ads.
www.youtube.com,m.youtube.com##+js(trusted-set-request-field, youtubei/v1/player, playbackContext.contentPlaybackContext.isInlinePlaybackNoAd, true)

! Pre-roll video ads on a HARD navigation. Blocking ad REQUESTS (added
! 2026-08-25) kills the tracking and serving calls but cannot touch the
! pre-roll itself: its stream comes from googlevideo.com, the same origin
! as the real content stream. And there is no client-side
! /youtubei/v1/player request to reshape on a hard nav — YouTube embeds
! the whole player response in the page HTML.
! test: on a cold load of a www.youtube.com/watch page that serves a
!   pre-roll, window.ytInitialPlayerResponse.adSlots is undefined (it was
!   an array of 4 when measured 2026-08-25) and no .ytp-ad-skip-button
!   appears; the content video plays from 0:00.
www.youtube.com,m.youtube.com##+js(trusted-prune-window-json, ytInitialPlayerResponse, adSlots, adPlacements, playerAds, adBreakHeartbeatParams)
