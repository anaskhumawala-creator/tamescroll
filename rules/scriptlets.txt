! tamescroll scriptlet rules (CC0) — engine-injected +js(...) filters that
! call our own clean-room scriptlets. Kept separate from the cosmetic
! rule files (youtube.txt etc.): these compile INTO the adblock engine,
! not the settings-pane surface layer.

! YouTube player request. Sets one field on the outbound /player request
! body; the player then starts the video without the multi-second stall
! owner reports #9/#12 describe. Runs only where the user has turned the
! site's Ads switch on.
! Provenance (clean-room): public protobuf write-up
! (https://iter.ca/post/yt-adblock/), not uBO/AdGuard code.
! test: on a youtube.com /watch SPA click, the outbound
!   POST /youtubei/v1/player body carries the field and the video plays.
www.youtube.com,m.youtube.com##+js(trusted-set-request-field, youtubei/v1/player, playbackContext.contentPlaybackContext.isInlinePlaybackNoAd, true)

! Desktop pages embed the first stream in ytInitialPlayerResponse; dropping
! `streamingData` makes the player fetch it through the request above,
! which brings a hard navigation in line with an SPA click (first frame
! ~8s instead of 24-37s, measured 2026-08-28). Ad-slot fields are removed
! alongside, as the public filter lists do.
!
! Trade: with no embedded fallback, a failed /player request shows
! YouTube's own error. Keep videoDetails and the rest intact.
!
! m.youtube embeds no stream (its page always fetches client-side), so
! only the ad-slot fields are removed there. Do not add streamingData to
! the mobile rule.
www.youtube.com##+js(trusted-prune-window-json, ytInitialPlayerResponse, streamingData, adSlots, adPlacements, playerAds, adBreakHeartbeatParams)
m.youtube.com##+js(trusted-prune-window-json, ytInitialPlayerResponse, adSlots, adPlacements, playerAds, adBreakHeartbeatParams)
