! Title: tamescroll — YouTube thumbnail blur (optional)
! Description: Blurs thumbnail images on the surfaces you reach on
!              purpose, while leaving titles fully readable.
! Version: 1
! Expires: 1 day
! License: CC0-1.0 (see rules/LICENSE)
!
! WHY THIS IS A SEPARATE FILE
!
! youtube.txt removes the algorithmic surfaces and is right for
! everybody. Blurring is a personal choice, so it subscribes separately.
! Someone who wants a quieter YouTube should not be forced into blur,
! and someone who wants blur should not have to edit a file to get it.
!
! WHY THIS BLURS SOME SURFACES AND NOT OTHERS
!
! Blurring every thumbnail everywhere makes YouTube unusable — you
! cannot find the video you deliberately came for, and an unusable tool
! gets abandoned, which puts the user back on the real YouTube.
!
! The algorithmic surfaces are not blurred here because youtube.txt has
! already removed them outright. There is nothing left to blur on the
! home feed, the Shorts shelves, the recommendation sidebar or the end
! screen. That is deliberate: removed beats blurred, because a blurred
! thumbnail still says "something is here" and still invites a look.
!
! What remains are the surfaces you reach on purpose — search and
! subscriptions. On those, images are blurred and titles are left
! completely readable, because titles are how a person finds content
! they chose and text is not the bait.
!
! THERE IS DELIBERATELY NO HOVER-TO-REVEAL
!
! A reveal gesture trains the un-blur reflex, which is the exact habit
! this is meant to break. If you need to see a thumbnail, open the
! video. Do not add a reveal rule to this file.
!
! NOTE ON :style()
! `:style()` is a uBlock Origin cosmetic extension, supported by uBO,
! AdGuard and Brave's adblock-rust. It is not part of plain EasyList
! syntax, so a very old or minimal blocker may ignore these lines
! rather than error.


! ============================================================
! SEARCH RESULTS
! ============================================================

! [live] ytd-search counted 1; ytd-thumbnail counted 4 on a search page
! test: on a search results page ->
!   getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('ytd-search ytd-thumbnail')).filter !== 'none'
youtube.com##ytd-search ytd-thumbnail:style(filter: blur(16px))
youtube.com##ytd-search yt-thumbnail-view-model:style(filter: blur(16px))


! ============================================================
! SUBSCRIPTIONS
! ============================================================

! [unverified] subscriptions page redirected to home on the test account
! test: on https://www.youtube.com/feed/subscriptions ->
!   getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('ytd-browse[page-subtype="subscriptions"] ytd-thumbnail')).filter !== 'none'
youtube.com##ytd-browse[page-subtype="subscriptions"] ytd-thumbnail:style(filter: blur(16px))
youtube.com##ytd-browse[page-subtype="subscriptions"] yt-thumbnail-view-model:style(filter: blur(16px))


! ============================================================
! CHANNEL PAGES
! ============================================================
! Reached on purpose, same treatment as search.

! [unverified]
! test: on any /@channel/videos page ->
!   getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('ytd-browse[page-subtype="channels"] ytd-thumbnail')).filter !== 'none'
youtube.com##ytd-browse[page-subtype="channels"] ytd-thumbnail:style(filter: blur(16px))
youtube.com##ytd-browse[page-subtype="channels"] yt-thumbnail-view-model:style(filter: blur(16px))
