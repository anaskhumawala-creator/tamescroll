! Title: tamescroll — Facebook
! Description: Removes Facebook's algorithmic surfaces — Reels, Watch,
!              Marketplace bait, and the "Suggested for you" units the
!              feed injects between the posts of people you follow.
! Version: 1
! Expires: 1 day
! License: CC0-1.0 (see rules/LICENSE)
!
! STATUS: EVERY RULE [unverified].
! Facebook signed out is a login wall and nothing else — measured
! 2026-08-28 in the app on both www.facebook.com and m.facebook.com
! (mobile UA): 0 links, 0 articles, 6 images, and aria-labels for
! nothing but the login form. There is no way to read the real DOM from
! this machine without a session, so every selector below is written
! from the structure Meta exposes to assistive technology and must be
! confirmed on a logged-in phone before any of it is tagged [live].
!
! WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROMISE: FEED ADS.
! uBlock Origin's maintainers stopped chasing Facebook's ads on
! 2026-08-10 — the "Sponsored" label is deliberately engineered so that
! no static selector can recognise it, and Meta watches the public
! blocklists to break whatever lands. We are not going to pretend to win
! a fight the people who invented this work have conceded. The vendored
! EasyList/uBO snapshots still apply whatever network-level blocking
! they carry; our own rules target the ALGORITHMIC SURFACES, which is
! what this app is actually about and which Meta cannot obfuscate
! without breaking its own navigation.
!
! ANCHORS, AND WHY THESE ONES
! Meta's class names are generated and rotate. Three things do not:
!   1. hrefs — /reel/, /watch, /marketplace are the app's own routes;
!   2. aria-label on navigation controls, because screen readers need
!      them (they ARE localised — an English UI is assumed, and a
!      non-English one degrades to no-op, never to a broken page);
!   3. role="article", the feed item container.


! ============================================================
! REELS — every entry point
! ============================================================

! Reels in the left rail / bottom bar.
! [unverified]
! test: signed in on facebook.com -> no Reels entry in the navigation;
!   Home, Friends, Profile and Menu all still present and clickable
facebook.com##a[href^="/reel/"]
facebook.com##a[href*="/reels/"]
facebook.com##div[aria-label="Reels"]

! Reels units injected between feed posts. The tray is a feed item that
! contains reel links and nothing else a normal post would carry.
! [unverified]
! test: scroll the home feed past a Reels tray -> tray gone, the posts
!   above and below it untouched
facebook.com##div[role="article"]:has(a[href^="/reel/"])


! ============================================================
! WATCH — the video-only algorithmic feed
! ============================================================

! [unverified]
! test: no Watch entry in the navigation; /watch typed by hand still
!   loads (we hide entry points, we never block navigation)
facebook.com##a[href^="/watch"]
facebook.com##div[aria-label="Video"]


! ============================================================
! MARKETPLACE — shopping bait in a social feed
! ============================================================

! !surface: marketplace | Marketplace | Shopping surfaced inside the feed
! [unverified]
! test: no Marketplace entry in the navigation
facebook.com##a[href^="/marketplace"]


! ============================================================
! SUGGESTED / PEOPLE YOU MAY KNOW — the algorithmic injections
! ============================================================

! !surface: suggested | Suggested posts | Posts from accounts you do not follow
! Facebook labels these units for screen readers, which is the only
! non-rotating hook they have.
! [unverified]
! test: scroll until a "Suggested for you" post appears -> hidden, and
!   the next real post from a followed account is still there
facebook.com##div[role="article"][aria-label*="Suggested"]

! !surface: pymk | People you may know | Friend suggestions in the feed
! [unverified]
! test: the People You May Know carousel is gone from the feed
facebook.com##div[aria-label="People you may know"]


! ============================================================
! NAGS
! ============================================================

! App-install interstitial on mobile web. Anchored on the intent:// or
! app-store outlink, the same hook that worked on m.youtube.
! [unverified]
! test: load m.facebook.com on a phone -> no "Open in app" bar
facebook.com##a[href^="intent://"]
facebook.com##a[href*="play.google.com/store/apps"]
