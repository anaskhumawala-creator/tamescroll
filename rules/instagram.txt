! Description: Removes Instagram's algorithmic surfaces — Reels and
!              Explore — while leaving the followed-accounts feed, DMs,
!              profiles and stories untouched.

! STATUS: PARTLY VERIFIED 2026-08-28.
! instagram.com/explore/ RENDERS SIGNED OUT under a mobile UA — measured
! in the app: 12 images (every one on scontent.cdninstagram.com), the
! navigation present with aria-labels Home / Direct / Reels / Search and
! explore / Profile, one a[href^="/reels"], one a[href^="/explore"].
! So the navigation rules below are [live]; anything that needs a FEED
! (suggested units, in-feed reels) still needs a logged-in session and
! stays [unverified].
! Meta ships obfuscated, frequently-rotated class names, so
! class-based selectors are useless — everything below anchors on
! hrefs and ARIA labels, which survive rotation. Verify each rule on a
! logged-in session and add [live] tags before flipping the Instagram
! tile to ready in the app (docs/plan.md: Instagram is "partial and
! honest about it").
!
! DESIGN
! The followed feed stays (it is the user's own choices — same call as
! Reddit's home feed). Reels and Explore are the algorithmic traps and
! go entirely. Suggested-for-you units inside the feed go when a
! reliable anchor is found — not expressible without the live DOM.


! ============================================================
! REELS — every entry point
! ============================================================

!surface: reels Reels

! Reels tab in the left/bottom navigation. The trailing slash was a
! GUESS and it was wrong: the live nav href is "/reels" with no slash,
! so the drafted selector matched nothing.
! [live] measured 2026-08-28, signed out, mobile UA, /explore/:
!   a[href^="/reels"] -> 1, [aria-label="Reels"] -> 1
! test: on https://www.instagram.com/explore/ ->
!   a[href^="/reels"] must be hidden; the Home, Direct, Search and
!   Profile controls must remain
instagram.com##a[href^="/reels"]

! Reels items surfacing inside other feeds (anchored on link).
! [unverified]
! test: scroll the home feed until a Reel unit appears ->
!   article :has(a[href^="/reel/"]) hidden while normal posts remain
instagram.com##article:has(> div a[href^="/reel/"])


! ============================================================
! EXPLORE — the algorithmic grid
! ============================================================

!surface: explore Explore grid

! Explore tab in the navigation. On the live mobile nav this control is
! labelled "Search and explore" and its href has no trailing slash --
! a[href="/explore/"] matched 0. Search stays reachable: hiding the
! entry point does not block navigation.
! [live] measured 2026-08-28: a[href^="/explore"] -> 1
! test: on https://www.instagram.com/explore/ ->
!   a[href^="/explore"] hidden, the grid links (a[href^="/popular"])
!   still present in the DOM
instagram.com##a[href^="/explore"]


! ============================================================
! NAGS
! ============================================================

!surface: nags App-install nags

! "Use the app" / app-install banners on mobile web.
! [unverified] — anchor on the App Store / Play Store outlinks
! test: load on a phone-sized viewport logged in ->
!   app-install banner absent
instagram.com##div:has(> a[href*="app.appsflyer.com"])
