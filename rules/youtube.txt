! Title: tamescroll — YouTube
! Description: Removes YouTube's algorithmic surfaces — home feed, Shorts,
!              recommendation sidebar, end-screen wall — while leaving
!              search, subscriptions, playlists and the player untouched.
! Version: 1
! Expires: 1 day
! License: CC0-1.0 (see rules/LICENSE)
! Homepage: https://github.com/tamescroll
!
! DESIGN RULES FOR THIS FILE — read before editing
!
! 1. A wrong selector that hides the video player is far worse than a
!    missed Shorts shelf. Prefer scoped, conservative selectors.
! 2. Every rule carries a `! test:` line saying how to tell it still
!    works. A rule with no stated test cannot be checked by the watcher
!    and must not be merged.
! 3. Scope page-specific rules with YouTube's own `page-subtype`
!    attribute on `ytd-browse`, never by guessing at paths.
! 4. Ads are NOT this file's job. The embedded engine (adblock-rust)
!    handles them via EasyList. The few ad rules below are belt-and-
!    braces for in-feed ad units that survive network blocking.
! 5. Comments are deliberately NOT hidden. They are not an algorithmic
!    trap and hiding them is a personal preference, not the mission.
!
! VERIFICATION STATUS (2026-08-18, youtube.com desktop, Chrome)
! Selectors marked [live] were counted in the real DOM this session.
! Selectors marked [unverified] are believed correct but the surface
! did not render on the test account (empty home feed, no Shorts shelf).
! Verify before relying on them.

! FEED SHELVES — the junk that is not the feed

!surface: home_shelves Feed shelves

! THE SHELVES NEEDED A SURFACE OF THEIR OWN, AND THIS IS WHY.
!
! Owner 2026-08-30: "breaking news still shows on the homepage", then
! "its not gone" after the rule shipped. His own diagnostics report
! explains it: kind "home", 58 images judged, FORTY of them 686px
! thumbnails, previews playing. A hidden home feed has no thumbnails to
! judge -- so his Home feed is SHOWN, deliberately, and every rule above
! is scoped to the `home` surface and correctly switched off. The rule
! was never wrong. It was unreachable.
!
! He wants the feed and not the shelf, and today that is not expressible:
! the only way to hide a shelf is to hide the whole feed with it. So the
! shelves get their own toggle, and it is independent of Home feed.
!
! SELF-LIMITING BY CONSTRUCTION. Every selector here is gated on :has()
! against a shelf, so it can only ever match a section that actually
! contains one -- the vertical video grid (ytm-rich-grid-renderer) is
! never matched, and if a name here does not exist the rule matches
! nothing rather than blanking his feed. That matters because the home
! feed cannot be rendered on this machine at all: signed out, m.youtube
! answers "Start watching videos to help us build a feed", so no
! selector below can be read off a live DOM here.
!
! VERIFIED ON A LIVE HOME FEED, 2026-08-31 (probe_home_shelf_live.py).
! Every earlier round recorded "signed out, m.youtube renders no feed".
! It does now -- 11 ytm-rich-item-renderers, 20 watch links, a chip bar --
! so the shelf could finally be measured instead of guessed at:
!   ytm-rich-section-renderer 2, and section 0 carries
!   ytm-rich-shelf-renderer 1, 371px tall, 14 video links, titled
!   "Breaking news -- Death toll rises to nearly ...". THAT IS HIS SHELF.
! With Home feed = Shown and this surface hidden: section 0 display none
!   and height 0, the shelf height 0, and ytm-rich-grid-renderer still
!   VISIBLE 1 of 1. The remaining rich items drop 11 -> 3 because eight
!   of them live inside the news shelf and the Shorts shelf, which is
!   the point.
! A SECTION IS A SHELF; AN ITEM IS A VIDEO. A second census of the live
! home feed (probe_home_census.py, a different load) settled the shape
! and made the first rule too narrow. Every row is one of three things:
!   ytm-rich-item-renderer     328px, 2 watch links  = one feed video
!   ytm-rich-section-renderer  a shelf -- 371px/14 watch links for
!                              Breaking news, 614px/4 shorts links for
!                              a Shorts shelf
!   ytm-continuation-item-renderer  32px, the load-more
! So the shelf test is the ELEMENT, not what happens to be inside it,
! and the first version -- gated on :has(ytm-rich-shelf-renderer) --
! would have missed any shelf built out of something else.
!
! The `:not(:has(a[href*="/shorts/"]))` is not caution, it is a toggle
! boundary: a Shorts shelf belongs to the Shorts surface, and hiding it
! from here as well would leave Shorts a dead toggle -- a failure this
! repo has already shipped twice.
! [live] ytm-rich-section-renderer on m.youtube home: Breaking news
!   (371px, 14 watch links, 0 shorts) and two Shorts shelves (614px,
!   0 watch links, 4 shorts links each)
! test: on m.youtube.com home in the Android app, Home feed = Shown ->
!   the video grid still renders; the Breaking news shelf does not
m.youtube.com##ytm-browse ytm-rich-section-renderer:not(:has(a[href*="/shorts/"]))

! Desktop, same surface and the same :has() guard, so a wrong name here
! costs nothing.
! [unverified] not read off a live desktop home feed
! test: on https://www.youtube.com/ with Home feed = Shown ->
!   ytd-rich-grid-renderer still renders; shelf sections do not
youtube.com##ytd-browse[page-subtype="home"] ytd-rich-section-renderer:has(ytd-rich-shelf-renderer)
