Terms of use

Last updated 23 October 2026

By installing or using tamescroll you agree to these terms. They are short because the app is simple: it is free, it has no accounts, and it keeps nothing about you.

What tamescroll is

tamescroll is a web browser. It opens websites, including the platforms you choose, and changes how their pages look on your own screen: it hides parts of a page, blocks ads and blurs images. Every change happens on your device. tamescroll does not modify any platform's app, does not use their APIs, and does not download, copy or redistribute anyone's content.

Your accounts and the platforms' terms

When you sign in to YouTube, Reddit, X, Instagram or any other site in tamescroll, you do so on that site's own page, with your own account, and that site's terms apply to your account as they always have. You are responsible for how you use those accounts. tamescroll never sees, stores or sends your passwords.

Filters are a help, not a guarantee

The filters and the image and video blur work hard to hide what you asked them to hide, but no filter is perfect. Something may occasionally get through, or something harmless may be blurred. Platforms change their pages often, and a change can break a filter until tamescroll's rules are updated. Please do not rely on tamescroll as the only protection for a child or anyone else in your care.
