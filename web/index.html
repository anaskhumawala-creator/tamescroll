How it works
01

Go anywhere

Search the web or type any address, and keep the sites you use as tiles on the home screen. tamescroll never suggests a site to you.

02

The pull stays out

On the sites we tame, feeds, short-video shelves, recommendations and autoplay are hidden before you see them. Nothing comes back unless you switch it on.

03

Every site, your rules

A plain bar sits on top of every page: home, the address, and a shield. The shield opens that site's settings: blur on or off, ads on or off, and the show-or-hide switches for a tamed site.

A browser first

Any website opens in tamescroll, the same page your phone's browser would load. Share a link to tamescroll from any app and it lands in the address box, ready to open here instead of in a feed.

Pictures, your rules

Pictures and thumbnails can be blurred on your phone before you see them: all of them, a smart selection, or none. Blur inside playing videos is switched off for now while we make it better. Nothing you see is sent anywhere.

Free and open source

MPL-2.0-licensed and built in the open. Read the code, file an issue, or build it yourself. There is nothing to buy, nothing to subscribe to, and no account.

How tamescroll works with websites

Their site, shown calmly, on your device

tamescroll is a browser. It loads each site's own public web page and changes how that page looks on your screen. Like a browser's reader mode or a content blocker, it hides parts of the page before you see them.

Most sites need nothing more than the picture blur and, if you want it, ad blocking. A few sites are built around feeds, so we keep a set of rules for each of them that hides those parts too. Today those are YouTube, Reddit, X, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and they carry a small "Tamed" mark on your home screen. They are sites tamescroll works with, not the point of it.

  • Every change happens on your phone. Nothing is copied, saved, re-uploaded or sent to us. We have no server, no account system and no analytics.
  • We never touch anyone's app or code. tamescroll does not modify any other app, does not use any site's API, and does not download videos.
  • Creators' work is shown as published. Blur is a layer drawn over your screen, never an edit of the picture or video underneath.
  • The picture filter keeps nothing. It looks at what is on screen, decides whether to blur it, and forgets it. It does not identify anyone, store faces, or guess anything about a person beyond the single filter you chose.
  • You sign in with your own account, on the site's own sign-in page, and its terms apply to your account as they always have.
