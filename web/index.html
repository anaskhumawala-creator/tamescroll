How it works
01

Type your platforms

You name what you already use. tamescroll never lists or suggests one — a platform you don't type simply doesn't exist here.

02

Everything starts hidden

Ads, promoted posts, Shorts, Reels, For You, trending, autoplay — gone by default. Ads stay blocked unless you switch them back on for a platform.

03

Bring back what you choose

Comments, subscriptions, search — each surface has one plain toggle, per platform. Nothing returns unless you turn it on.

Cleaned platforms

Each platform opens as itself, minus what it uses to hold you: recommendations, feeds, Shorts and Reels. You add only the platforms you already use — tamescroll never suggests another.

Protects your gaze

Thumbnails and images are blurred on your phone before you see them. Video blur is in beta and starts off; turn it on from the player. Nothing you see is sent anywhere.

Free and open source

MPL-2.0-licensed, built in the open. Read the code, file an issue, or build it yourself. There is nothing to buy and nothing to subscribe to.

How tamescroll works with the platforms

Your view of their site, on your device

tamescroll shows you a platform's own website, the same page your phone's browser would load, and changes how that page looks on your screen. Like a browser's reader mode or a content blocker, it hides parts of the page before you see them: Shorts, Reels, recommendation feeds, autoplay and ads.

  • Every change happens on your phone. Nothing is copied, saved, re-uploaded or sent to us. We have no server, no account system and no analytics.
  • We never touch the platforms' apps or their code. tamescroll does not modify YouTube's or anyone else's app, does not use their APIs, and does not download videos.
  • Creators' videos play as published. Blur is a layer drawn over your screen, never an edit of the video, and the video you choose to play is never altered.
  • The image filter keeps nothing. It looks at what is on screen, decides whether to blur it, and forgets it. It does not identify anyone, store faces, or guess anything about a person beyond the single filter you chose.
  • You sign in with your own account, on the platform's own sign-in page, and its terms apply to your account as they always have.
