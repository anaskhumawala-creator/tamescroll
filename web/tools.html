Tools we like

tamescroll is one app for your phone. Other people are working toward the same thing, a calmer screen and platforms that stop pulling at you. Some of them do a part of it better than we do, so here they are.

All free to startNo affiliate linksChecked 27 October 2026
What do you want?
O
A calmer home screen

Olauncher

Your phone's home screen as a short list of words: no icons, no badges, nothing asking to be tapped. tamescroll can add its own icon there, so the cleaned platforms are the first thing you reach.

A cleaner YouTube on a computer

Unhook

Hides YouTube's recommendations, Shorts, comments and more, each with its own switch. The closest thing to tamescroll's YouTube tile on a computer.

No YouTube Shorts

YouTube Shorts Block

Does one thing: takes Shorts off YouTube's home, search and watch pages, or plays a Short as an ordinary video if you'd rather.

A cleaner X

Control Panel for Twitter

Keeps X on your Following timeline and hides the For You feed, trends and much of the clutter. It also works in Firefox and Edge on Android, and in Safari on iPhone.

HaramBlur

Blurs images and video on the page you're viewing, on your own device. It works on any website, not only the platforms tamescroll opens.

On a phone? Chrome and Safari on phones can't run most browser extensions. On Android, Firefox can, and that's how Control Panel for Twitter and HaramBlur get there. For YouTube, Reddit, X and Instagram on your phone, that's what tamescroll is for.
