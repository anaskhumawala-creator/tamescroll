Tools we like

Checked 27 October 2026

tamescroll is one app for your phone. Other people are working toward the same thing, a calmer screen and platforms that stop pulling at you, and some of them do a part of it better than we do. These are the ones we use or admire. None of them pays us, and we don't earn anything when you install them.

On your phone

Olauncher

Android · free · open source (GPL-3.0) · Google Play and F-Droid

A home screen of plain text: no icons, no badges, nothing asking to be tapped. It pairs well with tamescroll, which can put its own icon on your home screen so the cleaned platforms are the ones you reach first.

In your browser

tamescroll doesn't run on desktop yet. Until it does, these extensions cover a lot of the same ground.

Unhook

YouTube · Chrome, Firefox, Edge · free

Hides YouTube's recommendations, Shorts, comments and end screens, each with its own switch. The closest thing to tamescroll's YouTube tile on a computer.

Control Panel for Twitter

X · Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari · open source (MIT)

Keeps X on your Following timeline, and hides the For You feed, trends and much of the clutter. It also runs in Firefox on Android.

YouTube Shorts Block

YouTube · Firefox · free · open source (MIT)

Does one thing: takes Shorts off YouTube's home, search and watch pages, or plays a Short as an ordinary video if you'd rather.

HaramBlur

Images and video · Chrome, Firefox, Safari, plus Android and iPhone apps · open source (AGPL-3.0)

Blurs images and video on the page you are viewing, on your own device. It works on any website, not only the platforms tamescroll opens.

AdGuard

Ads and trackers · Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera · free extension

A thorough ad and tracker blocker for every site you visit. The browser extension is free and needs no account.
