Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

tamescroll collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no server of ours that your phone talks to. This page explains the few things that do leave your device, and where they go.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the platforms you add, your keyword list and the app's diagnostics log are stored only on your phone. Image and video filtering runs on your phone: the models look at what is on screen, decide whether to blur it, and keep nothing. No image, face or result of that analysis is stored or sent anywhere.

What leaves your device

  • The platforms you open. tamescroll loads YouTube, Reddit, X and the other sites you add exactly as a browser would. Those sites receive what any browser sends them, and if you sign in, their own privacy policies apply to your account.
  • Filter rule updates. The app fetches its filter rules, a small data file, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). GitHub sees your IP address when it serves the file, under GitHub's privacy statement. No identifier of yours is sent.
  • App updates, in the version downloaded from this site. That version checks the same place for a new release and downloads it from GitHub. The version from Google Play is updated by Google Play instead.
  • Diagnostics, only if you send them. Settings → About → Share hands the diagnostics log to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The log records how the filters performed and never contains web addresses.

Children

tamescroll does not collect data from anyone, including children. The platforms it opens have their own age requirements.

This website

This site has no analytics or cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address under Google's privacy policy, and it asks GitHub for the current download link, which GitHub sees the same way.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will change first, with a new date at the top. The app is open source, so every claim here can be checked in the code. Questions go to our issue tracker.

tamescroll is an independent, open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube, Reddit, X, Meta or TikTok.
