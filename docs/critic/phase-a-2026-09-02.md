### F1 — `cut-truth.mjs` mis-aligns scdet timestamps to the 10Hz grid by one sample, and the off-by-one **inverts** the knee the OTA ceiling was set from

- **SEVERITY** EXPOSURE
- **WHERE** `app/gaze/bench/cut-truth.mjs:74` (`Math.ceil(t * RATE)`); the numbers it
  produced are quoted in `app/gaze/src/scene-gate.mjs:96-110`,
  `app/gaze/src/tuning.mjs:40-46`, `app/gaze/test/scene-gate.test.mjs:80-108`.
- **CLAIM** A cut reported by scdet at time `t` is paired with the 10Hz delta at index
  `ceil(t*10)`. `fps=10` emits the source frame *nearest* each output slot, so the
  correct pairing is `round(t*10)`; `ceil` is one sample **late** whenever
  `frac(t*10) < 0.5`, i.e. about half the time, and then reads the post-cut steady
  frame (delta ~ 0) as "the delta at a cut". Measured on the same cached deltas over
  all ten corpus videos, 152,376 samples:

  | alignment | p05 at a cut | recall @28 | @50 | @60 | @75 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `ceil` (shipped), scdet 30 | **0.0** | 52.8% | 50.4% | 45.5% | 42.3% |
  | `round`, scdet 30 | **73.7** | 100.0% | 98.3% | 95.9% | 90.9% |
  | `round`, scdet 25 | 58.5 | 100.0% | 99.5% | **92.8%** | **50.0%** |

  `p05 = 0.0` at a labelled cut is the signature: 5% of "cuts" have a luma grid that
  did not change at all. The flat-looking recall curve in the shipped comment
  (52.8% -> 42.3% across a 2.7x threshold range) is the arithmetic of "half the rows
  are right and half are near-zero", not a property of the gate.

  Two conclusions written into shipped source are therefore false:
  1. *"the gate catches only about HALF of real cuts at ANY threshold — a cut between
     two similarly-lit shots is invisible to a 16x16 luma grid and always was."*
     Correctly aligned it catches 92.8-100% at 28-60.
  2. *"Past 75 recall falls off a cliff (42.3% -> 26.0% between 75 and 90)... There is
     no cliff up there to protect, which is what the old bound claimed."* Correctly
     aligned, the cliff is between **60 and 75** — recall halves, 92.8% -> 50.0%.
     `tuning.mjs` raised the OTA ceiling to 75 on that table, and `scene-gate.mjs`
     writes *"75 is where to go next if his rings say phantom did not move. This is an
     OTA dial, so that is one push, not a release."*
- **FALSIFIER**
  ```
  cd app/gaze/bench && SCDET=30 node cut-truth.mjs        # reproduces the shipped table
  # then change line 74 from Math.ceil(t * RATE) to Math.round(t * RATE) and re-run:
  SCDET=30 node cut-truth.mjs                             # recall @60 goes 45.5% -> 95.9%
  SCDET=25 node cut-truth.mjs                             # @60 92.8%, @75 50.0%
  ```
  Settled either way by whether `p05` of the AT-A-CUT column is ~0 (misaligned) or
  large (aligned).
- **COST** The shipped 60 survives (92.8% recall) — this does not impeach 1088. What it
  impeaches is the ceiling and the recommendation: a push to `CUT_DELTA` 75, which takes
  one OTA edit and reaches every installed phone in seconds, would halve cut recall. A
  missed cut is a stale CLEARED track surviving a shot change and absorbing the next
  person's observation — the mechanism this corpus's single biggest exposure was traced
  to. A woman sharp for a whole shot.

### F6 — ffmpeg's `scdet` is not an independent instrument; it is the same mean-absolute-LUMA measurement our gate makes, and it is blind to the exact class of cut the comment blames the gate for missing

- **SEVERITY** WRONG-NUMBER
- **WHERE** `app/gaze/bench/cut-truth.mjs:9-14` and `app/gaze/test/scene-gate.test.mjs:73-76`
  — *"an independent instrument, its own colour space and its own algorithm, knowing
  nothing about our 16x16 luma grid"*.
- **CLAIM** `scdet` scores the luma plane only. A hard cut from pure red to pure green —
  a maximal chroma change, luma 69 -> 70 — scores **0.391**, i.e. invisible:
  ```
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "color=c=0xFF0000:s=320x240:r=10:d=2" \
         -f lavfi -i "color=c=0x008200:s=320x240:r=10:d=2" \
         -filter_complex "[0:v][1:v]concat=n=2:v=1" -pix_fmt yuv420p chroma.mp4
  ffmpeg -i chroma.mp4 -vf scdet=threshold=0.1 -f null -   # score 0.391
  ```
  Over the corpus, `pearson(our 16x16 luma delta, scdet score) = 0.498` on all 152,376
  10Hz frames. So the two instruments share the blind spot the comment attributes to
  ours alone: *"a cut between two similarly-lit shots is invisible to a 16x16 luma
  grid"* — it is invisible to scdet too, so scdet cannot be the instrument that measures
  how many such cuts we miss. "Two instruments that agree are evidence" does not apply
  to two resolutions of the same statistic.
- **FALSIFIER** the two commands above.
- **COST** The independence argument is the load-bearing half of "two independent
  instruments point the same way". Without it, the whole CUT_DELTA recall case rests on
  one luma-difference measurement compared against itself at a different resolution —
  and (F1) misaligned.
