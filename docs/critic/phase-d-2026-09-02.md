## D1 — EXPOSURE. The `[1.33, 3.0]` clamp floor is non-protective at every cadence at or below ~1503ms, and `VERDICT_MAX_INTERVAL_MS` on the same OTA channel reaches 1200

**The claim.** `tuning.mjs` (this commit) and `setCoastPasses`'s
docstring both justify the floor as a measured protection decision:

> At his 2000 the cap is 1.33 \* 2000 = 2660ms and 1.0 really does reach
> 2000ms, costing **+16.0s (man) and +10.0s (woman)** of exposure against
> the shipped value. **THAT is what the floor refuses**, measured rather
> than assumed.

`tuning.test.mjs` pins it with an assertion message saying the floor
*"refuses the 2000ms window"*.

**It refuses it at `told = 2000` and nowhere below ~1503.** The coast is

```
cap     = max(PTRACK_MAX_COAST_MS 2000, round(passes * told))
blurred = min(cap, max(PTRACK_MAX_MISS_BLURRED_MS 900, round(2.5 * told)))
```

so the `passes` term only reaches the answer while `passes * told > 2000`.
At `passes = 1.33` that needs `told > 1504`. Measured against the shipped
module, not the formula:

```
told   shipped(2)  OTA floor 1.33   raw 1.0 (what the floor refuses)
1000         2000            2000                2000   <<< FLOOR NON-PROTECTIVE
1200         2400            2000                2000   <<< FLOOR NON-PROTECTIVE
1500         3000            2000                2000   <<< FLOOR NON-PROTECTIVE
1600         3200            2128                2000
2000         4000            2660                2000
3000         6000            3990                3000
```

**And the corpus prices it.** At `told = 1500`, k=3, the value the clamp
calls the safe floor produces the identical row to the value the clamp
exists to refuse — in both gender arms:

```
$ GENDER=man   K=3 TOLD=1500 node bench/coast-ab.mjs 1.0,1.33,1.5,2
passes   coast   EXPOSURE  FALSECOVER   PHANTOM  births
1       2000ms      38.0s      132.5s    346.0s    212
1.33    2000ms      38.0s      132.5s    346.0s    212
2       3000ms      25.5s      139.0s    465.0s    174   <- SHIPPED

$ GENDER=woman K=3 TOLD=1500 node bench/coast-ab.mjs 1.0,1.33,1.5,2
1       2000ms      36.5s      185.0s    399.0s    211
1.33    2000ms      36.5s      185.0s    399.0s    211
2       3000ms      30.0s      194.5s    545.5s    178   <- SHIPPED
```

**+12.5s (man) and +6.5s (woman) of exposure, delivered by a value
`tuning.mjs` calls safe.**

**How that regime is reached, three ways, none of them exotic.**

1. `VERDICT_MAX_INTERVAL_MS` is on the **same OTA channel**, clamped
   `[1200, 4000]`. One `rules/tuning.json` carrying
   `{"VERDICT_MAX_INTERVAL_MS": 1200, "PTRACK_MIN_COAST_PASSES": 1.33}`
   has both values inside both clamps and lands the coast at 2000ms.
   §13a already recommends the 2000 -> 1200-1500 move ("Still worth
   doing"), so this is the combination the repo is currently pointed at.
2. `effZoom = min(VERDICT_MAX_INTERVAL_MS, max(ZOOM_INTERVAL_MS,
   lastVerdictMs * VERDICT_DUTY))`. Any device whose verdict costs
   250-375ms lands `told` in 1000-1500 with **nothing pushed at all**.
   His Redmi at 1250ms/verdict is cap-pinned at 2000; a faster phone is
   not, and the whole point of an OTA channel is that it reaches phones
   that are not his.
3. Below `told = 800` the dial is completely **inert** (coast is
   `max(900, 2.5*told)` whatever `passes` is), so on desktop a push does
   nothing — safe, but it means the dial's behaviour is a step function
   of a number nobody reads back (D9).

**The defect in one line: the protected quantity is the coast in
milliseconds, and the clamp is expressed in passes.** The real floor on
the coast is `PTRACK_MAX_COAST_MS = 2000`, which `coast-ab.mjs`'s own
header states correctly — and 2000ms is exactly the window §15a measures
as costing +16.0s/+10.0s.

**FALSIFIER TO CLOSE.** Either clamp the derived coast (refuse a push
whose `passes * effZoom` would fall below the measured floor at the
cadence actually in force), or re-derive the floor as a function of
`told` and say in `tuning.mjs` that the guarantee holds only at
`told >= 1504`. A row in §15a at `told = 1500` would also close it by
making the cost of the joint push explicit.
